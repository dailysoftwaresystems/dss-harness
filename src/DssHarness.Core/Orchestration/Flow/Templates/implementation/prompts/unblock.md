## The answer

{source.text}

Your last report said what you could not do, what stood in the way and what you needed. Your notes and that report
are in what you were given. The answer above is the person's: what you are to do instead, or what they did to lift
what stood in the way.

## What to do

1. Read the answer against what you said. Where it settles what stood in the way, do what you could not do before.
2. Where it tells you to do something else instead, do that. Where that needs a path outside the ones your task
   allows, list it again as what you cannot do: the engine refuses that write.
3. Where it leaves a point open, decide as the answer would have you, and say in your summary what you assumed.
4. Build and run your tests as before. Where you cannot, say why in what you ran.

## What to return

The same report as before, brought up to date, every list in full: what you did about the answer, and what you ran and
what it showed. List as what you cannot do only what still cannot be done after the answer, each with what stands in
the way and what would let it be done: the person is asked again for it.

When you report, the engine goes on with your work as it would have: it tests it, folds it and checks it.
